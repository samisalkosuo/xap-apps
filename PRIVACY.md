# XAP Apps Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to me. XAP Apps are designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

XAP Apps do not require registration or the creation of a user account. I do not operate servers that collect or store personal information from the apps, and I do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Information created or used by an application, such as application settings, captured content, or location information when a feature requires it, remains on your device or, where applicable, in your own iCloud account.

XAP Apps do not include third-party advertising or analytics tracking. Any permissions requested by an application, such as access to the camera, photos, or location, are used only to provide the functionality that requires that permission.

Some XAP Apps may offer optional purchases through Apple's App Store. These purchases are handled by Apple using the standard App Store purchasing services. I do not receive or store your payment information.

XAP Apps is a personal hobby project, and respecting user privacy is part of keeping the applications simple and focused.

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of XAP Apps changes. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
